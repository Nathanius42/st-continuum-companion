## Star Trek: Continuum Remastered

The ship data, ship and faction icons, emblems, logos, sounds, trailer thumbnails and the animated logo in `app/assets` come from *Star Trek: Continuum Remastered*, a Homeworld Remastered mod by the Continuum team, and are used with the team's blessing. The turntable renders in `app/assets/turntables` are renders of the mod's ship models, whose makers are credited on each ship's page in the app and in `data/credits.source.json`. None of this is covered by the MIT licence.

## Fan art

The homeworld scenes in `app/assets/factions` were generated for this project, in the manner of *Star Trek: Starfleet Command II*'s empire cityscapes. They are fan art, shared for non-commercial viewing alongside the code, and are not covered by the MIT licence.

## Fonts

League Gothic and Pathway Gothic One are under the SIL Open Font License (`app/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`).

The Star Trek fan fonts the released app also uses (Trek Movie, Trek Classic, Klingon Dagger, Romulan Eagle and the faction scripts) are not in this repository, since their terms don't allow passing the font files on. A build from this repository draws those headings in the platform font instead; the downloads on the Releases page include them.
